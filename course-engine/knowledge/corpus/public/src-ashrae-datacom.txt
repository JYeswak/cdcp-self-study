TC 9.9
was formed to address concerns of TC 9.9 Mission Critical Facilities, technology spaces and electronic equipment, designs, operations, maintenance, and efficient energy usage of modern data centers and technology spaces.
TC9.9’s
Mission Statement:
To be recognized by ALL areas of the datacom industry as the unbiased engineering leader in HVAC and an effective provider of technical information for the datacom industry.
ASHRAE TC 9.9 Datacom Encyclopedia
The ASHRAE TC 9.9 Datacom Encyclopedia evolved in 2024 from the longstanding ASHRAE Datacom Series, a series of print books that provides information on data center related subjects. Over time, the pertinent information from all the books in this series will transition to be housed in this online encyclopedia, which will replace the book series completely. This comprehensive online encyclopedia collects essential knowledge about important datacom topics such as facility design considerations, ITE design considerations, environmental guidelines, cooling technologies, and energy efficiency in a central hub, providing on-demand access to frequently updated datacom-related content anytime, on any device, anywhere. Access to the encyclopedia is granted via payment of an annual subscription fee.

ASHRAE Datacom Series
The print books of the ASHRAE Datacom Series – a staple in the data center industry for nearly two decades – provided comprehensive treatment of data center cooling and related subjects. The pertinent information from many of the books has already been moved to the
ASHRAE TC 9.9 Datacom Encyclopedia
, and over time all the books in this series will transition to the online encyclopedia. (The
ASHRAE TC 9.9 Datacom Encyclopedia
also houses PDFs of every edition of the 14 books in the ASHRAE Datacom Series for historical reference.) The ASHRAE Datacom Series books that have not yet been incorporated into the online encyclopedia are still available in print and PDF format in the ASHRAE Bookstore:
Structural and Vibration Guidelines for Datacom Equipment Centers (Book 5)
Particulate and Gaseous Contamination in Datacom Environments, Second Edition (Book 8)
Real-Time Energy Consumption Measurements in Data Centers (Book 9)
Green Tips for Data Centers (Book 10)
PUE™: A Comprehensive Examination of the Metric (Book 11)
IT Equipment Design Impact on Data Center Solutions (Book 13)
Advancing DCIM with IT Equipment Integration (Book 14)
