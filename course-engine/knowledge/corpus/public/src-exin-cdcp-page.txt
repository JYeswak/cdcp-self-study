EXIN EPI Certified Data Centre Professional
EXIN EPI® CDCP® is designed to test a candidate’s understanding of the key components of data centres. This certification addresses how to set up and improve key operations and maintenance aspects that are required to ensure high availability.
Get certified
Certification information
Who is this certification for?
Data Centre Professional (CDCP)  has been created with IT facility and data centre operations professionals in mind. It is ideal for those who work in and around the data centre and are responsible for improving availability and manageability.
