## Should-fail and limits

I initially marked **m06-q091** convertible because a power-quality list can
become a troubleshooting scenario. I rejected it on closer reading: the item
supplies no incident facts or source-backed response mapping, so choosing a
remedy would invent content. It is U, not an easy C.

This proxy cannot decide whether a proposed scenario is genuinely encountered
by a data-centre professional, whether the existing citation supports the
exact decision threshold, or whether the resulting item would require
analysis rather than recognition. It cannot adjudicate truth or
discrimination. Definitions can often be embedded in a scenario, but that
does not make the scenario sourced; legal/AHJ items are especially
jurisdiction-sensitive. The 396 C calls mean “worth a human conversion
draft,” not “safe to rewrite.”

No item was rewritten and no credential or certification claim is made.
