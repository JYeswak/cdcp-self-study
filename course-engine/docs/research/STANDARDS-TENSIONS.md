# STANDARDS TENSIONS — teach carefully

| Tension | Correct teaching resolution |
|---------|----------------------------|
| **Uptime Tier vs TIA Rated** | Different organizations and programs. Do **not** say “Tier III = Rated 3.” Use each brand’s language. |
| **ISO/IEC 22237 vs EN 50600** | Related facilities/infra families (intl vs European). Structure is comparable; do not claim identical clauses without source. |
| **ASHRAE recommended vs allowable** | Recommended ≈ preferred operational envelope; allowable ≈ wider equipment survival range. Don’t collapse them. |
| **Code (NFPA/AHJ) vs voluntary standard** | Codes can be enforceable; many SDO standards are voluntary unless adopted. **AHJ wins.** |
| **OCP efficiency culture vs enterprise Tier marketing** | Hyperscale optimizes cost/PUE/deployment speed; Tier marketing optimizes topology narratives. Different goals. |
| **CDCP awareness vs design engineer competence** | Course depth is **awareness / interview-ready**, not licensed PE design. |

Items that touch these tensions must not assert false equivalence.
