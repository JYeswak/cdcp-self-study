Heat path — chip → outdoors

Interview one-liner: every watt to IT becomes heat that must leave the building, or equipment fails.

STEP 1 / 5

Chip / IT load

Servers convert electrical energy almost entirely into sensible heat. Density (kW/rack) sets how fast temperature rises if cooling stops.

STEP 2 / 5

Air or liquid at the rack

Cold aisle / rear-door HX / direct-to-chip / immersion — first transport of heat away from silicon.

STEP 3 / 5

Room terminals (CRAC / CRAH / in-row)

Precision units move heat into refrigerant or chilled-water loops. Containment keeps streams from mixing.

STEP 4 / 5

Plant (chillers, pumps, towers)

Central plant rejects heat toward the outdoor environment; economizers help when weather allows.

STEP 5 / 5

Outdoors

Ultimate sink. Anything that breaks this chain — power loss to plant, valve fail, blocked airflow — is a cooling incident.
