### 3.1 Proper lock-out/tag-out procedures → `.2.4`

Source: C-147, C-333. Do **not** write another 147-vs-Subpart-S trivia stem (`m15-q223` already exists). The hole is the **procedure**.

| Claim | Clause |
|---|---|
| An energy isolating device is a mechanical device that physically prevents transmission or release of energy. Push buttons, selector switches, and other control-circuit devices are **not** energy isolating devices. | 1910.147(b) *Energy isolating device* |
| Control-circuit devices (push buttons, selector switches, interlocks) may **not** be the sole means of deenergizing. Interlocks are **not** a substitute for lockout and tagging. | 1910.333(b)(2)(ii)(B) |
| Application sequence: prepare (know type/magnitude) → shutdown → isolate every needed device → affix lockout/tagout → relieve stored/residual energy → **verify** isolation before work. | 1910.147(d)(1)–(6) |
| If stored energy can reaccumulate to a hazardous level, verification of isolation continues until the work is done or that possibility ends. | 1910.147(d)(5)(ii) |
| Before treating circuits as deenergized, a qualified person tests the parts to be exposed **and** checks for induced voltage or unrelated **backfeed**. | 1910.333(b)(2)(iv)(B) |
| On-site employer and outside (contractor) employer **inform each other** of their respective lockout/tagout procedures. | 1910.147(f)(2) |
| Shift or personnel changes require **specific** procedures that keep lockout/tagout continuous, including orderly transfer between off-going and oncoming employees. | 1910.147(f)(4) |
| Electrical-utilization exposure is excluded from 1910.147 and sits in Subpart S. Use this only as a *boundary* on a procedure stem, not as a new citation quiz. | 1910.147(a)(1)(ii)(D) |
