# VERDICT — Luna

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Research-only conclusion; no implementation and no certification claim.**

1. The live repository is an honest original study corpus, but its perfect sittings measure recognition more than specialist performance.
2. The headline result is `287/287`, not evidence that a learner can defend a facility design or diagnose a live failure.
3. What is real: the strongest items separate Rated, Tier, Availability Class, and nines instead of inventing a crosswalk.
4. What is real: path traces, last-common-point/SPOF reasoning, catcher topology, W-class semantics, and recommended-versus-allowable distinctions.
5. What is real: the 2026 cause framing refuses an invented outage pie and treats people/process as contributors rather than a universal root cause.
6. The active contradiction is still `PRACTICE Q4` / `mock40-q04`, which teaches the retired human-error cartoon beside `m01-q210`.
7. The bank is 846 items, 821 approved, 25 retired, and 846 `qualitative_only`; breadth is not calculation depth.
8. A real CDCS-depth item must expose assumptions and make the learner calculate, draw, select, or reject a plausible engineering option.
9. The first missing calculation family is battery autonomy: load, temperature, aging, discharge behavior, generator start, and vendor curve.
10. The first missing drawing family is one-line isolation: identify the last common point, bypass source, energized boundary, and concurrent-maintenance fiction.
11. UPS parallel questions must include synchronization, control/common-mode failure, load sharing, impedance, and bad-module isolation.
12. Generator questions must include synchronizing, kW/kVAR sharing, reverse power/VAR protection, master sync, cold-start testing, and manual fallback.
13. Cooling questions must connect CFM/CMH, `ΔT`, sensible/latent load, psychrometric state, humidity ratio, and coil approach rather than reciting a factor.
14. Fire questions must distinguish agent mechanism, concentration/quantity, discharge calculation, detection/interlock sequence, room integrity, and hold time.
15. EMF questions must distinguish electric from magnetic fields and make distance, geometry, frequency, measurement, and shielding tradeoffs explicit.
16. Fuel questions must treat usable burn time, day tanks, return routing, contamination, polishing, spill controls, and local requirements as one availability chain.
17. A Fluidstack-style oral would expose these gaps immediately, especially when a candidate is handed a one-line or a number and asked what assumption makes it true.
18. Assembly is a second failure surface: hard items exist, but seed 7 and parts of seed 42 can miss the catcher/W-class/path/nines cluster entirely.
19. The correct research priority is a small, sourced, assumption-labeled specialist set plus an assembly coverage floor; another definition-only expansion will not solve it.
20. Bottom line: the curriculum foundation is worth keeping, the current score is overinterpreted, the calc/drawing holes are material, and completing this program does not certify anyone.
