# Module 10 — Water Supply (folder)

**Primary lesson (study this first):** [`../10-water.md`](../10-water.md)

## Covered here (via primary notes)

- Why water is mission-critical for many cooling plants
- Makeup, blowdown, humidification, fire water (usually separate)
- Backup strategies: tanks, dual feeds, wells, reclaimed, dry design-out
- WUE metric; quality/chemistry (scale, corrosion, Legionella programs)

## Self-check (no peeking)

1. Evaporative plants without makeup lose cooling capacity — looks like a “cooling outage.”  
2. Fire water and process water are usually **separate** systems.  
3. Water-light designs trade water risk for electrical/ambient limits.
